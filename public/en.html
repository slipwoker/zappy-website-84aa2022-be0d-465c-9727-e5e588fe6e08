Advanced capabilities

Dedicated professional divisions for difficult access, facade treatment, nano coating, stain and graffiti removal, ecological pest control, ozone disinfection, laboratory and clean room cleaning. We combine manpower with advanced technical expertise to provide comprehensive solutions for all your organization's maintenance and cleaning requirements.

Proces provides a complete service envelope for building management and maintenance, with precise adaptation to client needs. We combine professional manpower with advanced technological capabilities, providing continuous and reliable service to regular clients alongside dedicated and rapid response for one-time projects.

We operate dedicated departments for routine work and special projects, so each project receives the required attention and expertise. Our field teams undergo rigorous training and are equipped with the best technology, from reverse osmosis systems to specialized drones for facade cleaning.

Whether it's an annual maintenance contract for an office building or a one-time deep clean after renovation, we tailor the methodology and team to every project.

The Proces expert team is ready to build a personalized service plan for you – from routine cleaning to advanced high-altitude work with a drone. Leave your details and we'll get back to you with a professional proposal.

Height access methods

Rappelling, lifting platforms, lifts, scaffolding, and drone with water cannon – we reach every point.

Advanced facade treatment

Window cleaning with reverse osmosis, dirt-repelling nano coating, and graffiti removal with sand or glass blasting.

היגיינה מתמחה

Laboratory, clean room, and factory disinfection with ozone gas, alongside thorough cleaning after construction or renovation.
